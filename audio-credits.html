Broken Genesis Audio Credits

The game uses CC0 1.0 music and effects, plus gunshot samples under the separate license noted below. The audio files are bundled with the game. Music is encoded for the web, and some battle cues change tempo or begin later in the recording.

Story and menu music

  • Earth uses Overworld by IntelligentGene (Another Page Studio).
  • Shiva uses Great Labyrinth by MintoDog.
  • Ifrit uses Dark Place by SkyleTheFrench.
  • Menu and battle selections include music from Helice Incredible Adventure by Komiku (Loyalty Freak Music).

Battle music

  • JRPG Epic Rock Battle Theme #1 by HydroGene.
  • Hope, Heavy Battle 2, Hard Battle 1, and Hard Battle 2 by MintoDog.
  • Battle RPG Theme by Cleyton Kauffman.
  • The Final Battle by skrjablin.
  • Cynic Battle Loop, a looped version of Battle Theme A by cynicmusic.

Sound effects

  • SpritePilot combat, magic, weapon, movement, and creature effects.
  • Kenney Impact Sounds, sourced from Untamed Pixels’ 150 Combat Sound Effects collection.
  • Gunshot Sounds by Vincent Sevedge (Tabasco), copyright 2009, licensed under CC BY 3.0. Edited into short single-shot cues and converted to MP3.
